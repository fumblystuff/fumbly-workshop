My router table came with a set of the [Rockler Snap-Lock Insert Rings](https://www.rockler.com/snap-lock-insert-rings){target="_blank"} and enjoy having a full set of them when I use my router table. I inadvertently damaged one of them a while ago, and I never got around to looking for a replacement. 

Hopping on the Rockler web site, I quickly found that Rockler does sell individual insert rings (I never imagined they would), so I drove over and picked up the one I needed. When I got it home, I quickly realized that Rockler had changed the color on the rings, the new one was a completely different color than the others. 

![Rockler Snap-Lock Insert Rings](/images/2026/rockler-snap-lock/rockler-snap-lock-inserts.jpg)
{% imageCaption "rockler-snap-lock-inserts" "Rockler Snap-Lock Insert Rings" %}

Its not a big deal, I was just surprised they would do that considering their brand color.

Anyway, my router table has 6 rings and the ring set that came with it only has slots for 5 rings. Apparently the 1.5 inch ring came with the lifter assembly. When I switch a ring out to use a smaller or bigger one, and put it back in the box, the rings get all out of order. On top of that, I needed slots for 6 rings, not the 5 included with the set, and the cardboard slots were falling apart after more than two years of use.

I decided to design my own 3D printed insert ring holder; here's an image of the first attempt. 

![Tinkercad designing ring holder v1](/images/2026/rockler-snap-lock/router-table-insert-holder-01.png)
{% imageCaption "router-table-insert-holder-01" "Tinkercad designing ring holder v1" %}

After I printed that unit, and it was a lot of printing, I realized that I'd gone too far with my design. I intended for this to go inside the existing box from Rockler, but then I didn't need so much height to the unit. I decided to make this available to people who wanted something to sit on a counter, or sit in the tray on the router table. 

![An image of the insert holder v1](/images/2026/rockler-snap-lock/unit-1.jpg)
{% imageCaption "unit-1" "Insert holder version 1" %}

I published the design file, you can find it at [Rockler Snap-Lock Insert Ring Holder v1](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/65sWFkpNVaC-rockler-snap-lock-insert-ring-holder){target="_blank"}

I then made a v2 version that cut out most of the central portion of the unit as shown below.

![Tinkercad designing ring holder v2](/images/2026/rockler-snap-lock/router-table-insert-holder-02.png)
{% imageCaption "router-table-insert-holder-02" "Tinkercad designing ring holder v2" %}

Here it is printed and stocked.

![An image of the insert holder v2](/images/2026/rockler-snap-lock/unit-2.jpg)
{% imageCaption "unit-2" "Insert Holder v1" %}

The beauty of this is that it fits inside the existing box, with a slot for one more ring.
